Hoops workout builder

Build custom shooting workouts on your computer and copy them to the Hoops app on your UNA Watch.
Up to 10 workouts of up to 8 steps each. Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded.

How to install

  1. Import your current workouts.bin first. The file you download replaces the whole custom workout list on the watch, so anything not in this page is lost. (The built-in workouts are not affected.)
  2. Edit the workouts below, then press Download workouts.bin.
  3. Watch: make sure Hoops is not running, connect the watch over USB (it appears as a drive) and copy the file to Apps/Hoops/workouts.bin, replacing the old one. Eject the drive, then open Hoops.
  4. Simulator: with the simulator closed, copy the file to Software/Output/workouts.bin.

Workouts are numbered in list order when you download, so moving or deleting one renumbers the ones after it; past History entries show the name of the workout that now has their number. workouts.json is a readable copy (the same format as the app's built-in preset list) that you can import here again.

Limits: 10 workouts, 8 steps per workout, names up to 19 characters (plain letters, digits and punctuation; the watch font has no accents), values 1–999 (shots, or seconds for Time steps), rest 0–255 s. A session stops counting at 400 shots. Time estimate: 12 s per shot plus rests and timed steps.
